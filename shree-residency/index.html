Shree Residency

A G+7 tower on a 42 × 32 m plot — 28 flats, stilt parking, a mandir, children's play area, and a terrace pool deck with gym and community hall. Walk through any flat, ride up to any floor, watch it build stage by stage, and read every dimension off the drawings.

  • 28 flats · G+7
  • 30.0 × 17.0 m tower
  • 25.2 m to parapet
  • 17 stilt car bays

01 — Walk it

Two ways through the same building

Orbit the whole scheme and scrub a ten-stage build sequence — that's the 4D. Then step inside and walk it in first person: walls stop you, doors open, and every flat is furnished. Switch between the stilt, any of the seven floors, and the terrace deck.

WALK

First person, with collision

Joystick or arrow keys, drag to look. You can't walk through walls — the doorways are cut from the same drawings.

LEVEL

Stilt to terrace

Nine levels. The furnished interior follows you up, so the view from the 6th floor is the real one.

STAGE

10 build stages

Site and compound, stilt, each of the seven floors, then the terrace. One tap plays the whole sequence.

LIGHT

Day and evening

Switch the sun. Evening shows the massing the way an elevation never can.
